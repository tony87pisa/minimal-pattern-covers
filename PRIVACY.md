# Privacy Policy — Minimal Pattern Studio / Autonomy Pin Publisher

Last updated: 2026-09-22

This application is a personal automation tool operated by the account owner ("we").

## What the app does
- Creates and schedules Pinterest Pins on the owner's own boards to promote the owner's digital products.
- Runs only on the owner's Pinterest account; it does not act on behalf of other users.

## Data we handle
- Pinterest OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's machine and are used only to call the official Pinterest API.
- We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal data of Pinterest users.
- No analytics, tracking, or profiling of third parties is performed.

## Data retention
- No third-party personal data is retained. Tokens can be revoked at any time from Pinterest account settings.
